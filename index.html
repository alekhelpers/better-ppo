FAQ

Straight answers

Does Better PPO sell my information?

No. Filter answers stay on this page. A callback is shared only with the partner you chose, and only if you ask.

Does Better PPO sell insurance or give financial advice?

No. This is a matcher. Licensed professionals confirm what fits your state and situation.

Do I pay Better PPO?

No. There is no fee to use the matcher.

How do you choose partners?

We only list people we would send a friend to. If a topic has no confirmed partner yet, the page says so.

What if there is no partner in my state?

We tell you. Nothing you typed is stored or shared.

Privacy

Preferred partners. Not a lead list.

We work with preferred providers we trust. We do not sell or distribute your information. Your answers are only used to help match you with the right professional. You are free to contact them directly.

Nothing you type is stored by this page. If you later choose “Request a call from this partner,” the note you write is shared only with that partner, and only because you asked.

Better PPO is a matcher. It does not sell insurance, give tax, legal, or investment advice, or promise a price. A licensed professional should confirm what actually fits your state and situation.
